Next to the day-to-day editorial work for SBOL, we have been working on something special for you as the SBOL community. 
We asked ourselves: how can we offer deeper insights into SBOL and our organisation while making it easier for the community to follow our ongoing work? 
Our answer is **SBOLinside**.

SBOLinside is a video podcast format specifically focusing on providing inside stories from our work on SBOL and our organization. 
The series will feature interviews with key figures driving the standard forward, including members of the Steering Committee, the Editors, the Chair, and many more.
We will also present what we are currently working on and share ways on how you can actively engage with and support the Synthetic Biology Open Language (SBOL). 
The episodes of SBOLinside will be released on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@syntheticbiologyopenlangua6204) and announced on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sbol/) and our [blog](https://sbolstandard.org/blog/).

Is there a specific aspect of SBOL you would love to learn more about? Reach out to us and we will be happy to consider your topic for a future episode.
