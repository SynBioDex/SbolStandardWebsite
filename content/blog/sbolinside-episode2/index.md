## Episode 2: How Do We Integrate Data into SBOL?

In this episode, host **Erik Kubaczka** welcomes **Prof. Anil Wipat**, AI Director at Prozomix and Professor at Newcastle University. Having served as the second chair of SBOL starting in late 2015, Anil brings a unique perspective spanning molecular microbiology, bioinformatics, computing, and commercial biotechnology.

Here is a brief sneak peek of what Anil discusses in this episode:

* **Transition to Open Governance:** How the community established an elected steering committee and introduced SBOL Enhancement Proposals (SEPs)— an open, RFC-style proposal process that empowered anyone to propose improvements and directly shape the standard.
* **SBOL 1 to SBOL 2 (and Joining COMBINE):** How SBOL expanded beyond basic sequences and components to capture molecular interactions and functions, while formally joining COMBINE to connect with the broader bioinformatics and biotechnology standards ecosystem.
* **Simplifying Towards SBOL 3:** Why the transition toward SBOL 3 addressed community feedback about steep learning curves by streamlining data models and APIs, making it significantly easier for software developers to build tools.
* **Standards as Contracts for AI Agents:** Drawing from his current industry work in enzyme engineering, Anil shares a forward-looking vision of how standards like SBOL can serve as communication contracts for autonomous AI agents collaborating across the Design-Build-Test-Learn cycle.
